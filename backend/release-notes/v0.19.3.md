This release focuses on making Scanopy comfortable to use on a phone and on making plans, billing, and licensing easier to understand at a glance.

## New Features

- Mark paid capabilities with a crown instead of scattered upgrade prompts, so it's obvious at a glance what your plan includes.
- Let invited users complete sign-up even when open registration is disabled.

## Improvements

- Make modals, tables, toasts, tagging, and the license key field usable at phone width, including list-then-detail navigation for configuration screens.
- Tidy up Settings billing so each fact appears once, and reveal the Servers card and install hint only after you have a license key.
- Require a verified email before issuing license keys

## Bug Fixes

- Fix SNMPv3 discovery against devices whose engines report a boot counter of zero.
- Keep the Settings entry visible in the sidebar on mobile, and show Plans on self-hosted Community installs.
- Fix option popovers closing immediately when opened by tap on touch devices.
- Fix the overlap between the options panel and the minimap button in topology view.
