This release focuses on making Scanopy comfortable to use on a phone and on making plans, billing, and licensing easier to understand at a glance.

## New Features

- Mark paid capabilities with a crown instead of scattered upgrade prompts, so it's obvious at a glance what your plan includes.
- Add Apple device logos, theme-aware branding, and curved dependency links for a cleaner topology view.
- Let invited users complete sign-up even when open registration is disabled.
- Add a daemon count metric broken down by daemon version, making it easy to spot daemons that need upgrading.

## Improvements

- Make modals, tables, toasts, tagging, and the license key field usable at phone width, including list-then-detail navigation for configuration screens.
- Show each plan's limits directly on the plan cards and name the trial length on the call to action, so you can compare plans without guesswork.
- Tidy up Settings billing so each fact appears once, and reveal the Servers card and install hint only after you have a license key.
- Require a verified email before issuing license keys and block disposable email domains at sign-up.
- Rewrite the Free topology gate to explain what each topology view adds and show scan status in its own panel.
- Speed up first load by skipping requests your role can't make and smoothing out layout rendering.

## Bug Fixes

- Fix SNMPv3 discovery against devices whose engines report a boot counter of zero.
- Keep the Settings entry visible in the sidebar, and show Plans on self-hosted Community installs.
- Fix option popovers closing immediately when opened by tap on touch devices.
- Correct plan selection targets so they match the plans offered by the backend.
- Fix port assignment for client probes so probes no longer conflict over ports.
- Fix the overlap between the options panel and the minimap button in topology view.
