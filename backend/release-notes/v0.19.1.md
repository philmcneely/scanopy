This release sharpens how Scanopy recognises what's on your network and how that network reads on screen, with deeper device fingerprinting and a cleaner topology layout.

## New Features

- Add libvirt (KVM) support as a hypervisor, including manual assignment of guests to their host.
- Identify Apple devices through their lockdown service, reporting exact model and OS — including iPadOS.
- Capture TLS certificates during scanning and use issuer details to identify services that would otherwise look generic.
- Recognise a much wider range of consumer and smart-home hardware, including Kasa plugs, Bose and Cast speakers, smart TVs, myQ openers, Brother printers, mesh access points, Pulse shade hubs and Schlage locks.

## Improvements

- Rework topology layouts so Layer 2 hosts are ordered by switch port, Layer 3 elements are grouped by device type, and cards sit closer to what they connect to.
- Show a device's status alongside its detail at the same zoom level, so you no longer have to zoom twice to read a card.
- Redesign the credential and daemon setup wizards with numbered, carded steps, clearer field groups, and assigned hosts shown directly on each card.
- Require a target when creating a credential, and flag existing credentials that aren't assigned to anything.
- Make mDNS discovery more thorough by querying every relevant service type and repeating queries within the listening window, so more devices are found on the first pass.
- Warn when Proxmox guests exist that the scan can't reach, so missing VMs are no longer a mystery.
- Include the answering SNMP version in SNMP warnings, making credential mismatches easier to diagnose.

## Bug Fixes

- Fix stray IP addresses that were detached from the devices they belong to.
- Stop overly broad service rules from producing false positives, including devices incorrectly identified as switches.
- Fix popover lists scrolling the page behind them, and keep the options panel from being covered by the minimap expand button.
- Fix service detection for Spotify Connect and for greetings that arrive split across packets.
- Restore logos in PNG topology exports and keep logo badges correctly shaped and proportioned.
- Update bundled dependencies to address security advisories.
